# ONE SWEEP — sound notes

Written **before any note of the score**; updated when the coordinator moved the timeline to 78.2 s (CUES in
`src/shots.js` are the only source of times; every time in `render.py` is cue arithmetic, quantised to the picture frame).

The client's whole brief about the last films: "music was off the mood", cheerful music on epic moments. So the score is
written against this map, not the other way round.

Key: **C minor** until `CUES.sweep` (Aeolian, Phrygian b2 only as a bare fifth or a bass note, chromatic minor chords
Cm / Abm / Fm / Ebm for menace). **No major triad before the sweep.** Comedy is made with cuts and silence, never with a
jolly tune. Scale of the "level" column: 0 = digital silence, 1 = only tiny foley, 3 = quiet dread, 5 = heavy music,
7 = landing, 8 = hook, 8.5 = blade hit, 10 = the sweep. Only `CUES.sweep` may reach 10.

## Rules taken from the map
1. Only hook, landing, blade hit, boom and the beam are allowed to be big, and all stay under the sweep.
2. Every big-to-tiny jump is a hard cut on the picture frame with the reverb tails cut too.
3. Before the sweep harmony is only minor triads, bare fifths and clusters. No major triad, no dance rhythm.
4. Real samples for all music (VSCO, VCSL); oscillators only for designed SFX. Broom must be real bristles.

See `README.md` for the render, and `tools/analyse.py` for the proofs (`audio/analysis.json`, `audio/sound_env.png`).
